# Delivery Performance & Customer Satisfaction Root-Cause Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes the **Olist Brazilian e-commerce dataset** to understand what drives customer satisfaction and identify the major causes of poor customer experiences.

The analysis combines **Python, SQL, statistical analysis, AI-assisted review analysis, and Power BI** to investigate delivery performance, customer reviews, seller performance, product categories, complaints, and regional patterns.

### Business Question

> **What actually drives a customer's good or bad purchasing experience?**

The goal is not only to report sales, but to identify customer pain points and provide actionable recommendations for improving the overall customer experience.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The project investigates:

- What factors are associated with low customer satisfaction?
- Is late delivery associated with poor reviews?
- Which product categories have weaker customer satisfaction?
- Which sellers are underperforming?
- Are higher-value orders associated with lower ratings?
- Which regions show weaker customer experience?
- What are the most common customer complaints?
- Which high-revenue areas should the business prioritize?
- What actions can improve delivery performance and customer satisfaction?

---

## 📊 Dataset

The analysis uses the **Olist Brazilian E-commerce Dataset**, containing information about:

- Orders
- Customers
- Sellers
- Products
- Payments
- Order items
- Customer reviews
- Geographic information
- Product category translations

The dataset contains approximately **99,000 orders** and **100,000+ related records** across multiple tables.
